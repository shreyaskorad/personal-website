Work in learning science, behavior science, organizational design, career development keeps returning to the same test: does the design improve what people notice, choose, and correct in real conditions?

In this post, Baldwin & Ford (1988) is the baseline reference and canonical frame for transfer and maintenance of learned capability.

The mechanism is combined value: the same interaction has to help users do the job while also making them feel more in control, less alone, or more confident.

The risk is shipping purely functional usefulness and calling it enough, even though the product still gives users no reason to care about it.
