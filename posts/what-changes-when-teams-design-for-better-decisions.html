What changes when teams design for better decisions

19 April 2026 · 2 min read · modern-l-d, work, learning-design

Teams rarely need more more visible activity. They need a workflow that makes better decisions in live work easier to notice and reinforce.

Teams discuss the right topic, but execution quality stays flat because interventions are not anchored to repeat decisions in live work.

The mechanism sits inside the point in the workflow where people choose what to do next, not outside it.

A design can look successful on the surface and still weaken the next decision that actually shapes performance where the work actually happens.
